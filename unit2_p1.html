① CONNECT · Explore the Network

Read the connections carefully. You will need them in the next missions.

adopt

verb

Meaning: start using or accepting an idea, plan or way of doing something.

Collocations: adopt a policy · adopt a solution · adopt a greener lifestyle

Family: adoption · adoptive

Related: take up

Many students are adopting greener habits.

set up

phrasal verb

Meaning: establish or create an organisation, group, project or system.

Collocations: set up a club · set up a campaign · set up a project

Related: establish · create

The students set up an environmental club.

reduce

verb

Meaning: make something smaller in amount, size or degree.

Collocations: reduce waste · reduce pollution · reduce emissions

Family: reduction

Synonym: decrease · Opposite: increase

We should reduce the amount of plastic we use.

clean up

phrasal verb

Meaning: make a place clean by removing rubbish or dirt.

Collocations: clean up streets · clean up beaches · clean up a park

Related noun: clean-up

Students cleaned up the school yard.

protect

verb

Meaning: keep someone or something safe from harm or damage.

Collocations: protect the environment · protect wildlife · protect natural resources

Family: protection · protective

Synonym: safeguard · Opposite: damage

We need to protect endangered animals.

environment

noun

Meaning: the natural world around people, animals and plants.

Collocations: protect the environment · environmental problems · environmental protection

Family: environmental · environmentally

Related: nature · ecosystem

Everyone has a role in protecting the environment.

awareness

noun

Meaning: knowledge or understanding of an issue or situation.

Collocations: raise awareness · environmental awareness · public awareness

Family: aware · unaware

Pattern: be aware of something

The campaign aims to raise environmental awareness.

human

adjective / noun

Meaning: relating to people; a person.

Collocations: human beings · human activities · human health · human impact

Related: humans · humanity

Human activities can affect the environment.
